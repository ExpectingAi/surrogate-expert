How surrogate compensation works in Arizona

There is no single guaranteed amount that applies to every surrogate in Arizona. Offers can vary by program, prior surrogacy experience, individual circumstances, contract terms and included benefits. Compare the entire written package rather than relying on one advertised number.

What may be included in a compensation package?

  • Base compensation
  • Monthly or milestone-based allowances
  • Approved pregnancy-related expenses and reimbursements
  • Travel, childcare or lost-wage provisions when applicable
  • Insurance-related costs where the agreement provides for them
  • Additional contract-specific payments for defined events

What can affect an offer?

Prior surrogacy experience, location, program structure, medical circumstances, insurance and the negotiated agreement can all affect the package. A higher headline figure is not automatically a better package if important benefits or protections are missing.

How are payments handled?

Compensation timing should be described in the gestational carrier agreement. Programs commonly use an escrow arrangement or another documented payment process. Review the schedule, expense rules and any conditions with your independent attorney before signing.
