Surrogacy laws by state

A state-by-state starting point for women considering becoming a gestational surrogate. The colors below describe the type of legal framework — they are not a ranking of which states are “best.”

How to read the colors: A green state is not automatically suitable for every journey, and a red state does not mean every surrogacy-related arrangement is impossible. Eligibility, enforceability and parentage can depend on the agreement, family structure, genetic connection, residence, medical procedures, delivery location and effective dates of the law. Reviewed August 9, 2026.
Comprehensive statutory frameworkA detailed statutory pathway governs gestational surrogacy for qualifying arrangements.
Limited / fact-dependent frameworkThe state has a narrower statute, general parentage law, case law, or significant fact-specific procedural questions.
Significant statutory restrictionsCurrent law materially restricts, voids, or prohibits relevant types of surrogacy agreements.
Recent major legal change / transitionThe state recently changed its framework or has an important effective-date transition that deserves extra attention.

The color is a navigation aid, not a legal conclusion. Open the state guide and confirm the current requirements with qualified reproductive counsel.
