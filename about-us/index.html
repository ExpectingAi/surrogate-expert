About us

Surrogacy, made clearer

We started Surrogacy Expert because too many women considering surrogacy were getting vague answers, scattered information, and pressure instead of honest guidance. We wanted to build something different.

Our approach

Every page on this site exists to answer one real question a surrogate-to-be might have — written in plain language, without sales pressure, and without pretending the journey is simpler than it is. We believe the best way to support someone through a major life decision is to be honest about every part of it: the medical process, the legal protections, the compensation, and yes, the risks too.

What we believe

Transparency first

Real numbers, real timelines, real answers — not vague marketing language.

You deserve your own advocate

Independent legal representation isn't optional in our approach — it's foundational.

No judgment, ever

Whatever brought you here — curiosity, financial goals, a personal connection to infertility — your reasons are your own.

Support doesn't end at matching

We believe in being present throughout the whole journey, not just the sign-up.

"Surrogacy is one of the most generous things a person can do for another family. It deserves a process that treats it — and you — with real respect."

Part of the Expecting.Ai family

Surrogacy Expert is built and supported by Expecting.Ai, a fertility platform connecting intended parents, surrogates, and egg donors through thoughtful technology and real human support.
