Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Information we collect

When you use Surrogacy Expert, we may collect information you provide directly, including your name, contact information, and details submitted through our application and eligibility forms. We may also collect limited technical information such as browser type and general usage data to improve the site.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, evaluate eligibility, communicate about the surrogacy process, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Sharing your information

Information may be shared with affiliated fertility clinics, legal counsel, and partner agencies as part of facilitating your surrogacy journey, and only as necessary for that purpose. We do not share your information for unrelated marketing purposes without your consent.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us, including secure storage and limited access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we encourage you to contact us with any concerns.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us directly.

Contact us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at info@expecting.ai.
