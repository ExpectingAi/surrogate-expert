How long does it take a surrogate to match with intended parents?

There is no fixed timeline. Some surrogates meet intended parents quickly, while others take longer because matching is about compatibility, not simply being next in line.

Short answer

Your timeline depends on whether your screening is complete, where you live, your compensation expectations, your preferences about intended parents, and whether both sides agree on major medical and legal decisions. A good program should not pressure you to accept the first profile you see.

What intended parents usually look at

  • Pregnancy and delivery history
  • Medical screening status
  • Location and travel needs
  • Compensation package
  • Views on embryo transfer, termination and multiples
  • Communication style and desired relationship
  • Availability for appointments and travel

What you should evaluate too

Matching is mutual. Ask why the intended parents chose surrogacy, what kind of relationship they want during pregnancy, who will attend appointments, how they think about difficult medical decisions, and how much communication they expect after birth.

Can you decline a match?

Yes. You should be able to decline a proposed match if it does not feel right. A respectful program should treat compatibility as a two-way decision and explain whether declining affects your place in the matching process.

What can speed up matching?

Completing medical records promptly, being clear about your preferences, responding quickly to introductions, and keeping compensation expectations aligned with your program can help. None of those should replace proper screening or informed consent.
