Surrogacy laws in Kentucky

Kentucky has a surrogacy statute, but it specifically addresses non-compensated arrangements, making the picture more nuanced than in clearly surrogacy-friendly states.

See if you qualify

Is compensated surrogacy legal in Kentucky?

This is genuinely nuanced. Kentucky's surrogacy statute explicitly addresses surrogacy arrangements without compensation beyond reasonable expenses, which has led to some legal uncertainty around fully compensated arrangements. In practice, surrogacy does take place in Kentucky, but the legal structure is more particular than in states with comprehensive compensated-surrogacy statutes.

Are pre-birth parentage orders available?

This depends heavily on the specific arrangement and court. Because of the statute's particular language, outcomes can be less predictable than in clearly surrogacy-friendly states.
