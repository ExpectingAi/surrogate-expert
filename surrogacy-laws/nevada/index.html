Surrogacy laws in Nevada

Nevada has a detailed statutory framework governing gestational-surrogacy agreements and parentage.

Classification note: Green means Nevada has a comprehensive statutory framework; it is not a guarantee that every arrangement qualifies.

What Nevada law addresses

Nevada law expressly addresses gestational agreements, eligibility and agreement requirements, legal representation and parentage procedures. Compensation may be addressed within a compliant agreement.

Parentage procedure

Nevada provides a statutory route for establishing intended-parent status in qualifying arrangements. Counsel should confirm the correct filing, timing and vital-record process for the facts of the case.

What this means for a surrogate

Focus on whether your agreement satisfies the statute rather than relying on a “surrogacy-friendly” label. Your attorney should review compensation, insurance, medical autonomy and parentage provisions before execution.
