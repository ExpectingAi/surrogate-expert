Surrogacy laws in Rhode Island

Rhode Island has a statute governing gestational-surrogacy agreements and parentage. Green describes the existence of a comprehensive statutory framework, not a guaranteed outcome for every arrangement.

Legal note: Educational information only. Confirm current eligibility, agreement requirements, compensation rules and parentage procedure with Rhode Island-licensed reproductive counsel.

What the statute covers

Rhode Island law provides an express framework for gestational-surrogacy agreements, including requirements for the parties and the agreement and a legal path for establishing parentage when the statutory conditions are met.

Compensation and expenses

Compensated gestational-surrogacy arrangements may be structured within the statutory framework. The agreement should clearly address compensation, reimbursements, insurance and payment administration.

Parentage procedure

The statute provides a parentage process for qualifying arrangements. The exact filing, timing and vital-record steps should be confirmed for the specific case rather than treated as automatic.

What to verify before signing

  • Eligibility of the parties under current law.
  • Agreement formalities and timing before medical procedures.
  • Independent legal representation.
  • Insurance, expenses and compensation terms.
  • Jurisdiction and the planned parentage proceeding.
