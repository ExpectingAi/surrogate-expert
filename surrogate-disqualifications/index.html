What can disqualify you from being a surrogate?

There is no single nationwide checklist, but clinics and agencies commonly decline or defer applicants when pregnancy history or current health creates added risk.

Updated August 2026. Final medical eligibility is determined by the fertility clinic and treating professionals.

Common reasons an applicant may be declined or deferred

  • No prior successful pregnancy and delivery.
  • A pregnancy history that suggests elevated obstetric risk.
  • Current smoking, nicotine, recreational drug use, or other substance concerns.
  • Uncontrolled medical conditions such as hypertension or diabetes.
  • Certain medications or medical conditions that require specialist review.
  • A BMI outside the specific program's accepted range.
  • Psychological or social circumstances that do not meet program screening standards.
  • Too little time since a recent delivery, depending on clinic policy.

Does a C-section automatically disqualify you?

No. Prior C-sections do not automatically disqualify every candidate. Programs review how many C-sections you have had, how you recovered, your overall obstetric history, and the fertility clinic's medical policy.

What about a premature birth?

A history of preterm birth can trigger closer review and may disqualify an applicant in some programs, especially when the prior delivery suggests a meaningful risk of recurrence. The gestational age, reason for the early delivery, and the rest of the pregnancy history matter.

Can government assistance disqualify you?

Receiving public benefits is not a universal medical disqualification. However, agencies may review financial stability and whether surrogate compensation could affect benefit eligibility. Applicants should ask the program and, when appropriate, a benefits or tax professional how compensation could affect their situation.

Important distinction

An agency can decline an applicant under its own policies even when another program might consider her. Conversely, passing an agency's initial screen does not guarantee medical clearance from the IVF clinic.
